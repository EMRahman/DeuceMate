# DeuceMate Support

Welcome to DeuceMate support! Find answers to common questions and learn how to get the most out of your tennis scoring companion.

---

## Getting Started

### How do I start a match?
1. Open DeuceMate on your Apple Watch
2. Tap "Start Match"
3. Select who serves first: "Me" or "Opponent"
4. Start scoring!

### How do I score a point?
- Swipe **up** to score a point for yourself
- Swipe **down** to score a point for your opponent
- Swipe **left** to undo the last point

### How do I customize controls?
Open Settings to customize match features such as point-outcome tracking, changeover compass, and announcements.

---

## Features

### Undo Function
Made a mistake? No problem!
- Swipe **left** to reverse the last point
- You can undo multiple times, all the way back to the start
- The complete game state (including server) is restored

### Side Change Reminders
DeuceMate shows visual prompts when you should:
- Change ends (after odd-numbered games)
- Consider changing balls (after typical intervals)

## Match Types

### Standard Match
- Games to 6, win by 2
- Tiebreak at 6-6 (first to 7 points, win by 2)
- Best of 3 or 5 sets

### Super Tiebreak
- First to 10 points, win by 2
- Often used as a deciding set in modern tennis

### Server Rotation
DeuceMate automatically tracks who should serve and switches at the correct times.

## Troubleshooting

### The app isn't responding
1. Force quit the app by pressing the side button and swiping right on DeuceMate
2. Reopen the app
3. If the issue persists, restart your Apple Watch

### Match state was lost
DeuceMate automatically saves your match when the app goes to the background. If you lost a match:
1. Make sure the app didn't crash (check recently opened apps)
2. Open the app again—it should restore your last match
3. If storage is full on your watch, the save may have failed

## Tennis Rules Reference

### Scoring Sequence
- **Points:** Love (0), Fifteen (15), Thirty (30), Forty (40)
- **Deuce:** Score is 40-40
- **Advantage:** One point ahead after deuce
- **Game:** Win by 2 points after deuce

### Set Scoring
- First to 6 games, win by 2
- Tiebreak at 6-6
- Tiebreak: First to 7 points, win by 2

### Super Tiebreak
- First to 10 points, win by 2
- Used as a deciding set

### Server Rotation
- Alternate server every game
- In tiebreaks, alternate every 2 points

### Side Changes
- After 1st, 3rd, 5th games (odd games)
- Every 6 points in a tiebreak

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does DeuceMate work offline?**
A: Core scoring, match history, and analysis work without an internet connection. Watch ↔ iPhone sync is local. Personal iCloud Drive backup and any sharing or AI hand-off you choose require the relevant Apple or third-party service.

**Q: Is my match data synced to the cloud?**
A: Your watch and iPhone sync directly over Apple's WatchConnectivity, with no developer-controlled server. When iCloud is available, the iPhone app also keeps an automatic archive in your personal iCloud Drive for fresh-install restore, with HealthKit measurements removed; the developer cannot access it. Data may separately leave your devices when you deliberately export or share it, and is then handled by the recipient or service you choose.

**Q: Can I track multiple matches?**
A: You score one match at a time on the Apple Watch, and every completed match is saved to an unlimited on-device history you can review on the iPhone companion app.

**Q: Does it work for doubles?**
A: DeuceMate tracks the score the same way for singles or doubles. You choose one side as "Me" and the other as "Opponent."

**Q: Can I use this for padel?**
A: Yes! Padel uses the same scoring system as tennis, so DeuceMate works perfectly.

**Q: Is there an iPhone version?**
A: Yes. DeuceMate ships as an iPhone app with a bundled Apple Watch app. You score on the watch; the iPhone companion gives you a live scoreboard, spoken score announcements, unlimited match history, and detailed statistics.

**Q: Are there any subscriptions or in-app purchases?**
A: No! DeuceMate is completely free — no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads, and no hidden costs.

**Q: Can I back up or move my match history to a file?**
A: Yes. In the iPhone app, go to **Settings → Backup & Transfer** and use **Export Match Archive** to save your whole history to a single full-fidelity file (it includes any heart rate, steps, distance, and calories you recorded). **Import Match Archive** brings a file back — on the same phone or a new one — and lets you choose how it combines with what's already there:
- **Merge** keeps your current matches and adds the ones from the file. If a match is in both, it keeps the more complete copy (so you won't lose health data or a finished result), and it restores any match you'd previously deleted if it's in the file.
- **Replace** swaps your iPhone archive for exactly what's in the file. Matches not in the file are removed from the iPhone (though any still on your Apple Watch can sync back), so the app asks you to confirm first.

This is a manual file you control, and is separate from the automatic iCloud backup.

**Q: How do I delete my data?**
A: In the iPhone app you can swipe to delete individual matches from your history. Deleting the app removes its on-device data; the iCloud backup lives in your own Apple ID account and can be managed in iOS Settings under your name → iCloud.
