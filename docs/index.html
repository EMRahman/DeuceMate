Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is DeuceMate really free?
Yes — completely free for unlimited match tracking. No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads, ever.
What do I need to use it?
An iPhone on iOS 17 or later and an Apple Watch on watchOS 9 or later (Series 4 or newer, SE, or any Ultra). You score on the watch; the iPhone is your live viewer and archive.
Does it work for doubles and padel?
Yes to both. Doubles includes full four-player service rotation. Padel shares tennis's game/set scoring, so DeuceMate keeps score for it too (some tennis-specific tips won't all apply).
Do I need the AI coaching feature to use the app?
Not at all. Scoring, live viewing and stats work on their own. The AI Coach is an optional one-tap export — you choose to hand your own match data to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any app via Share.
Is my data sent to a server?
No. The app has no servers and collects no data. Watch ↔ iPhone sync is on-device peer-to-peer. An optional backup of your archive can go to your own iCloud Drive (for fresh-install restore only) — never to us.
How do the spoken announcements work?
When enabled, your iPhone speaks the score umpire-style while the app is open on screen courtside, through the phone speaker or a Bluetooth speaker. They pause if the phone is locked or the app is backgrounded.
