# DeuceMate walkthrough — simplified script

**Design length: 3:00 (180 seconds), with sections numbered 1–16.** One clear story: start → score on either device → understand tennis logistics → win → review → friend’s report → AI context → overall progress → progress against Alice. Use these section numbers when referring to changes.

**Status: parked (4 November 2026).** Capture and rendering are paused because the native automation approach consumed too much of the usage budget. All generated clips, assets, capture helpers and pipeline files have been removed. This document is the retained brief, not a completed video.

If resumed, first agree a substantially more usage-efficient capture approach. Work on one requested section at a time and generate **MP4 only**, with consistent encoding for later stitching. Do not rebuild the full automation pipeline or generate later sections without a new request.

## Screen presentation

- Always show a real Watch or iPhone screen. Whole screens may sit side by side; zoom or pan each screen recording as one continuous image.
- Never extract charts, pills, legends, titles or controls and combine them with other cutouts. No detached insets, rearranged UI or redrawn values.
- Use native scrolling, menus and orientation changes. Keep the app’s layout and relative positions intact during every zoom.
- Let the UI tell the story. No marketing paragraphs or statistics headlines; use only short optional captions such as Watch, iPhone, Serve side, Next server and Later in the match.
- Every transition and zoom is inside the action budget. Viewing time is additional settled time; do not consume it with movement.

## The four scored points

Each gets **nine seconds**, on the device doing the input:

- **1s:** Swipe and let the score update.
- **2s:** View the outcome choices.
- **1s:** Tap the outcome.
- **2s:** View the ending-shot choices.
- **1s:** Tap S+1 or Rally.
- **2s:** View the settled score on both devices.

The two Watch points and two iPhone points therefore get **36 seconds**, rather than rushing through the menus. Enable iPhone Input, point tracking and detailed shot tracking before capture. Keep the Watch connected; the iPhone forwards its scoring input to it.

## Logistics and match continuity

- Serve side: right/deuce at an even point total, left/ad at an odd total. Show the real court indicator and server marker. After a regular game, show the other player serving next.
- At **6–1**, seven games have been completed: show the app’s players-change-ends reminder.
- A tiebreak starts with one serve from its first server, then two per server. Demonstrate the app’s **balls-change-ends** reminder after an odd point total (e.g. **3–2**) and **players-and-balls-change-ends** reminder at six points (**3–3**). These refer to moving the balls between ends, not replacing old balls with new ones.
- Use one new fictional match ending **6–1, 7–6**, with the second-set tiebreak **7–5**. Label skipped time briefly and retain every underlying point. Derive all statistics, prompt contents and both report perspectives from this match; do not reuse the prior edit’s 139-point totals or 6–4, 7–5 result.
- Tag this match with the existing Alice identity before capturing review screens. Overall Trends and Alice’s head-to-head/comparison must refer to the same archive. The current Trends screen has no opponent selector; section 16 uses the genuine Opponents and Compare screens.
- Use the actual native scoring/category/reminder screens. The report must be the genuine **Opponent’s Perspective** export: your win appears as your friend’s loss. Keep their perspective throughout; do not present your recorded Health data as theirs.
